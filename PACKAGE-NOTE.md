# P10 — Package Note (Fiverr-ready)

**Gig title:** I will turn your restaurant's food-delivery orders into a Power BI dashboard and best-sellers report

**What the client gets:** not a data dump — a working Power BI
dashboard (KPIs, monthly trend, category and top-dish views, peak
hours, channel and area slicers) plus a short best-sellers report
that says which dishes to push, when to staff up, and where delivery
is losing repeat orders. The client stops guessing what sells and
gets one page of "do this first".

## Tiers (Hashir's band — $65/$140 tier-led)

| Tier | Price | Delivery | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $20 | 2 days | Best-sellers report only (top-10 dishes, category table, 5 actions) from the client's order export, PDF |
| Standard | $65 | 4 days | Full Power BI dashboard (.pbix) from the model + DAX in this repo, built on the client's data + best-sellers report, 1 revision round |
| Premium | $140 | 6 days | Dashboard + report + 3-month trend and peak-hour staffing plan + delivery-time audit by area with a rider-allocation recommendation + reusable monthly refresh script, 2 revision rounds |

**What's NOT included:** the restaurant's own order data — the
client exports it (Foodpanda / app / POS export) and sends the file;
building the client's Foodpanda/POS integration (quoted separately);
ad campaign management (quoted on seeing the ad account).

**Delivery time note:** counts from the day the client uploads their
order export, not from order placement.

**Proof this service works:** this repo's sample — fictional Lahore
Food Co, 1,800 orders over Q3 2026, PKR 1,712,880 total revenue,
Chicken Karahi the top seller (PKR 195,500), lunch + dinner peaks
carrying 63.6% of orders, Johar Town the slowest delivery area
(39.3 min vs Gulberg 26.8), 25 automated checks passing. The sample
report is the demo the buyer sees before ordering.
